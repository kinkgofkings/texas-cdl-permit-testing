Texas 3-Step Air Leak Test

Failing this test is an automatic failure on the Texas DPS Skills Exam!

Supply Tank NORMAL (125 PSI)

LOW AIR WARNING (< 55 PSI)
Step 1: Applied Pressure Leakage

Shut engine off, turn key ON, press & hold foot brake pedal for 1 minute.

Must NOT lose more than 4 PSI in 1 minute (Combination Vehicle)!
Step 2: Low Air Warning Signal

Fan the brake pedal repeatedly to bleed down air tank pressure.

Warning light & buzzer MUST turn on at or before 55 PSI!
Step 3: Spring Brake Pop-Out

Continue pumping brake pedal down further to drain air.

Yellow parking valve button MUST pop out between 20 & 45 PSI!
