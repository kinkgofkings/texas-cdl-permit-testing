Texas 3-Step Air Brake Leak Test

Master this step-by-step procedure to pass the Texas CDL skills test.

1

Applied Pressure Leak Test

Build pressure to 120-145 PSI, turn engine OFF, turn key ON. Fully apply foot brake. Hold for 1 minute.
Max Leakage: Combination vehicle must not lose more than 4 PSI in 1 minute.

2

Low Air Warning Signal

Fan the foot brake pedal to reduce air tank pressure.
Activation: Low air warning light/buzzer must activate at or before 55 PSI (typically around 60 PSI).

3

Spring Brake Protection Valve Pop-Out

Continue fanning the foot brake pedal down.
Activation: Parking brake valve knob must pop OUT between 20 and 45 PSI.
